### 🧬 Clinical Research • 📊 Data • 🔬 Science • 💻 Technology

MedOne Research is a full-service **Clinical Research Organization (CRO)** supporting the development and delivery of high-quality clinical research across the Central and Eastern European (CEE) region.

With extensive experience in global clinical trials, we combine medical, scientific, clinical research, and regulatory expertise with strong local knowledge, flexibility, and a commitment to quality.

Our work is guided by a patient-centered approach and a shared objective with our Sponsors: supporting the advancement of therapeutic solutions through accurate, efficient, and high-quality clinical research.

---

## 🩺 Our Expertise

MedOne Research provides services across the clinical research lifecycle, including:

* 🏥 Clinical Operations
* 📋 Regulatory Affairs
* 🩺 Medical Monitoring
* 🛡️ Safety & Pharmacovigilance
* ✅ Quality Assurance
* 👥 Patient Recruitment
* 📊 Biostatistics & Statistical Analysis
* ✍️ Medical & Scientific Writing

## 💻 Technology & Data

This GitHub organization supports MedOne Research's work in **biostatistics, data science, statistical programming, research automation, clinical data analytics, and software development**.

It provides a controlled environment for the development and maintenance of analytical workflows, reusable tools, applications, and other technology supporting clinical and scientific research.
